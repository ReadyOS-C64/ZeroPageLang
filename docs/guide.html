The ZeroPage programming guide

Ten chapters from typed source to resident code, far records, and relocatable DLLs.

Chapters

1. Types, control flow, and imports

Read data layout, expressions, and diagnostics through the 6510's byte and word model.

2. C64 I/O and strings

Choose between direct screen writes, memory-mapped registers, and KERNAL services.

3. Inline 6510 assembly

Use source operands, follow the preservation wrapper, and call a ROM routine or terminal vector.

4. Images, placement, and zero page

Plan a PRG and its regions, including startup copies, bank gateways, and reserved zero page.

5. Arenas, pools, and records

Choose a visible-RAM arena, a fixed-block pool, or an REU arena and work with structured data.

6. Far data and materialized views

Transfer bytes, fetch typed records, or edit a working range in C64 RAM and flush it.

7. DLLs, caches, and activation

Build a first DLL, then follow it from disk to REU to a shared execution window.

8. DLL loading, ownership, and banking

Handle missing disk files, share dependencies, preserve state, and use RAM under ROM.

9. Frames, recursion, and interop

Follow data across static and stack calls, and understand the ReadyOS boundary.

10. Inspect, test, and compare

Read generated artifacts and verify execution on the host 6510 runner and VICE.

How to follow the guide

Start with the language chapter or jump directly to the hardware topic you need. Each chapter explains the runtime contract and links its examples and reference material. The intro gives a short tour. The setup page collects tool installation and build commands.
